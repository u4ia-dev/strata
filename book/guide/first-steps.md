# First steps

## Switch on

1. Switch the FM-1 on. The STRATA splash shows for two seconds.
2. **HOME** opens: the sound's name, its category, a few of its settings, and the **bloom** at the right, a flower of
   dots that opens and swirls as you play.
3. Strata brings back the sound you were playing when you switched off. A new unit starts on **INIT**, a plain sound
   to build from.

![HOME](../images/Home-2x.png)

> **Tip:** if a sound ever stops the unit at power-on, hold <kbd>HOME</kbd> while you switch on. Strata then starts
> without bringing that sound back.

## Play

1. Play the keys. Raise or lower MASTER for the level.
2. Press <kbd>OCT+</kbd> or <kbd>OCT−</kbd> to move the keys an octave (−3 to +3).
3. The keys play at one velocity (the FM-1's keys have none); a MIDI keyboard on USB plays with its own.

## Try other sounds

1. On HOME, turn **PRESETS**. A list opens: INIT first, then every sound A to Z.
2. Turn PRESETS to the one you want. Nothing loads yet.
3. Press <kbd>SAVE</kbd> twice, quickly. The sound loads and the list closes.

Left alone for five seconds, the list closes and nothing changes. Strata comes with eight starter sounds: SUPERSAW,
PLUCK, PAD, FM KEYS, SYNC LEAD, LADDER BASS, VOWEL PAD and OTT GROWL. <kbd>HOME</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> (hold HOME,
press OCT+) steps to the next sound at once; <kbd>HOME</kbd> + <kbd>OCT−</kbd> to the one before.

## Change the sound with the knobs

HOME's four knobs are the sound's **macros**, MACRO 1 to 4: each moves whatever the sound designer put on it. INIT's do
nothing until you give them a job ([Shaping a sound](shaping.md) shows how).

To change the sound itself, open a menu:

1. Press <kbd>EDIT</kbd>. OSC A's WAVE page opens.
2. Turn KNOB 1 (WT POS): the wave morphs through its table. Turn KNOB 2 (TABLE) for another table.
3. Turn **ALGORITHM** to step through OSC A's pages: WAVE, UNISON, SPREAD, PITCH, OUTPUT, then VALUES.
4. Turn **SELECT** to step to the next module: OSC B, SUB, NOISE, FILTER, MATRIX.
5. Press <kbd>ENV</kbd>, <kbd>LFO</kbd>, <kbd>FX</kbd> or <kbd>GLO</kbd> for the envelopes, the LFOs, the effects, the
   voice and the tempo. Each remembers the page you left it on.

A knob turned slowly moves its value in fine steps; turned quickly, in big ones. The value shows where the page draws
it, or along the bottom while the knob turns.

## Turn things on and off

Hold the menu button and press <kbd>OCT+</kbd> for on, <kbd>OCT−</kbd> for off:

- <kbd>EDIT</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> turns on the source or filter shown (OSC B, SUB, NOISE or FILTER start off).
- <kbd>ARP</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> turns the arpeggiator on; <kbd>SCL</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> the chords.

## Made a mistake?

Hold <kbd>SAVE</kbd> and press <kbd>OCT−</kbd> to **undo**; <kbd>SAVE</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> to redo. Strata keeps
the last eight steps: each knob gesture, each sound loaded.

To put a whole module back as it starts, hold its menu button and hold <kbd>OCT−</kbd> for five seconds: a countdown
shows from the second second, and letting go of OCT− stops it.

## Back home

Press <kbd>HOME</kbd> to go back to the sound's HOME page from anywhere. Press it twice for the SETTINGS.
