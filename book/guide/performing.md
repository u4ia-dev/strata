# Performing

## Bend, wheel, glide

Hold <kbd>GLO</kbd>: the **PERFORM** layer. KNOB 1 is a pitch bend (it springs back to the middle when you let go),
KNOB 2 the mod wheel, KNOB 3 GLIDE, KNOB 4 VOLUME. The keys still play.

![PERFORM, GLO held](../images/Perform-2x.png)

To keep PERFORM open with your hands free, press <kbd>GLO</kbd> twice (or <kbd>GLO</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>). A tap on
<kbd>GLO</kbd> closes it.

## The macros

HOME's four knobs are MACRO 1 to 4. A good sound puts its most expressive moves on them: brightness, movement, space.
Hold <kbd>FX</kbd> to reach them from any page.

## Scale

Press <kbd>SCL</kbd>. KNOB 1 picks a SCALE (MAJOR, MINOR, DORIAN, the pentatonics, BLUES ..), KNOB 2 its ROOT, KNOB 3
how the keys FIT it:

- **OFF**: each key plays its own note; the scale's notes glow on the keys' LEDs.
- **SNAP**: a key off the scale plays the scale's note below it.
- **WHITE**: the white keys walk the scale from the root at C4; the black keys are silent.

Hold <kbd>SCL</kbd> and press a key to make that key's note the root. KNOB 4 sets the keys' VELOCITY.

## Chords

Press <kbd>SCL</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> and every key plays a chord. SELECT to CHORD to set them:

- **MODE KEY**: each key plays its chord in the scale (TRIAD, 7TH, 9TH, SUS2 ..: KNOB 2).
- **MODE FREE**: each key plays the chord TYPE picks (MAJ, MIN7, 9, 13 ..) on its own note.
- **VOICING** keeps chords close together (AUTO) or inverts them; **SPREAD** opens them up.
- **STRUM** (ALGORITHM): the notes one after another, up or down, with a human feel.
- **BASS**: the chord's root an octave or two under it.

<kbd>SCL</kbd> + <kbd>OCT−</kbd> (or <kbd>SCL</kbd> twice) turns the chords off.

## The arpeggiator

Hold a chord and press <kbd>ARP</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>: the ARP plays it in steps on the tempo. <kbd>ARP</kbd> +
<kbd>OCT−</kbd> turns it off; <kbd>ARP</kbd> twice turns it on or off too.

![The ARP's STYLE page](../images/PerfArp-2x.png)

Press <kbd>ARP</kbd> for its pages:

- **STYLE**: KNOB 1 the STYLE: **ARP** (notes one after another: RATE, MODE such as UP, UP-DOWN, RANDOM, and
  OCTAVES), **PATTERN** (the chord as rhythmic stabs, one of 16 patterns), or **HARP** (the keys become strings of the
  chord: run a finger along them).
- **GROOVE**: GATE (note length), SWING, LENGTH (how many of the 16 steps play) and LATCH (it plays on after you let
  go).
- **STEPS** and **RATCHET** (SELECT): each of the 16 steps can play, tie or rest, with its own level, octave, repeats,
  chance and slide.

## Tempo

The tempo is on <kbd>GLO</kbd>, SELECT to GLOBAL: BPM (20 to 300) and the time signature. To tap it: hold
<kbd>GLO</kbd> and tap <kbd>PLAY</kbd> three times or more, in time. Or set CLOCK IN to MIDI and follow a computer's
clock.

<kbd>PLAY</kbd> starts the transport: the ARP and PATTERN then step on its beat, and the looper plays.
