# Settings and the LEDs

## The SETTINGS

Press <kbd>HOME</kbd> twice. ALGORITHM steps the pages; <kbd>HOME</kbd> leaves.

![SETTINGS > SCREEN](../images/SetScreen-2x.png)

- **SCREEN**: the COLOR theme (twelve: STRATA, CLASSIC, ICE, VIOLET, PAPER, NIGHT, HI-CON, MONO, MOSS, DUSK, SAND,
  NEON), HELP (a hint as a module opens) and LIGHTS (how bright the LEDs are: OFF, LOW, MED, HIGH).
- **PANEL**: ACCEL (the knobs' speed sensitivity) and HOLD (how long a button is held before its layer opens).
- **MIDI**: the CHANNEL Strata listens on (ANY or 1 to 16), MIDI LEARN, CLEAR (forget the learned controllers) and
  PROGRAM (program changes load presets).
- **POWER**: SHOW BATTERY (on HOME or on every screen) and SCREEN OFF AFTER (darken the screen after 1, 5 or 15
  minutes untouched).
- **WIPE**: every setting back to the firmware's. Your presets and projects stay. Turn KNOB 1: it asks first;
  <kbd>SAVE</kbd> confirms.

The settings are kept by themselves, a few seconds after you stop playing.

## MIDI LEARN

1. SETTINGS > MIDI, turn LEARN on.
2. Go to any page and turn a knob: its parameter is picked.
3. Move a knob or fader on your MIDI controller. That controller now sets the parameter.
4. <kbd>OCT+</kbd> (or LEARN off) ends it.

## What the LEDs say

Every LED in Strata keeps to one code:

| The LED | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Off | nothing there |
| Glowing (dim) | something is there: a clip, a module in the chain, a note of the scale, a drum |
| Mid | in use: the scale's root, an accented drum, the playhead's step |
| Lit | active: a key held, a clip playing, the menu shown, the beat |
| Blinking | waiting: a launched clip, a count-in, REC waiting for the bar |
| Breathing | recording, or a layer held open |

- The menu buttons: the one whose menu is shown is lit, the others glow.
- <kbd>REC</kbd>: lit while the looper records; blinking while it counts in or waits for the bar.
- <kbd>PLAY</kbd>: lit for the first quarter of each beat while the transport runs; its green LED stays lit while it
  runs.
- <kbd>ARP</kbd>: lit while the arpeggiator is on, glowing while it is off.
- The keys: the scale's notes glow (the root brighter); on a drum track, the drums; on the GRID, the row's hits.

## DEBUG

Hold <kbd>HOME</kbd> for five seconds (past the help map) for the **DEBUG** menu: BENCH (what the FM-1 measures), KEY
SCAN (the panel's switches, live) and CORE 2 (the second core, off by default). They are for testing; the Manual's
[SETTINGS and DEBUG](../manual/settings.md) explains them.
