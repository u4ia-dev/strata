# Drums basics

Any looper track can be a **drum track**. It plays the project's **drum kit**: eight drum rows (KICK, SNARE, CLAP,
HAT CL, HAT OP, CYM, WT 1, WT 2) and a ninth, silent row, **DUCK**, that makes the synth duck out of the way.

## Make a drum track

1. Press <kbd>SEQ</kbd> (LOOP, CLIPS) and pick a track with PRESETS.
2. Turn KNOB 1 (TYPE) to **DRUMS** and stop turning. If the track has clips, Strata asks first: <kbd>SAVE</kbd>
   changes it, anything else keeps it.

## Play the drums

With the drum track selected, the keys play the kit:

- White keys 1 to 8 (F3 to F4): KICK, SNARE, CLAP, HAT CL, HAT OP, CYM, WT 1, WT 2.
- White keys 9 to 16 (G4 to G5): the same drums, **accented**.
- The first black key (F#3): DUCK.

Record them as you record notes: <kbd>REC</kbd>, play, <kbd>REC</kbd>.

## HOME on a drum track

![HOME on a drum track](../images/DrumHome-2x.png)

HOME shows the kit's name and its eight drums, each lit as it sounds. Its knobs move the whole kit: TUNE, DECAY, TONE,
and DUCK (how deep the synth ducks). PRESETS picks a drum (framed) for EDIT to open.

## Change a drum

Press <kbd>EDIT</kbd>. **DRUM** opens on the drum PRESETS picks (stopped, the drum you play is picked too).

![DRUM's MODEL page](../images/DrumModel-2x.png)

- **MODEL**: KNOB 1 picks one of seven models (ANALOG, PUNCH, FM, CHIP, NOISE, METAL, WAVE), KNOB 2 its TYPE. Any row
  can be any model: a CHIP kick, an FM snare.
- **SOUND**: TUNE, DECAY, TONE and the model's own knob.
- **DRIVE**, **EQ**, **SHAPE**, **MIX**: each drum's own drive, EQ, attack and body, level and pan.

SELECT to **KIT** for the whole kit: ACCENT, the DUCK and its CURVE, and COMP on all eight drums.

## Drum presets and kits

- <kbd>EDIT</kbd> twice on DRUM opens the **drum presets** of the row's category (KICK, SNARE, CLAP, HAT, CYMBAL, TOM,
  PERC, FX; eight factory presets each). SELECT steps the category, PRESETS picks, <kbd>SAVE</kbd> twice copies it into
  the row.
- <kbd>EDIT</kbd> twice on KIT or on HOME opens the **kits**: 808, 909, LO-FI, VIDEO GAME, TRAP, DRUM & BASS, then
  your own. <kbd>SAVE</kbd> twice loads one as the project's kit (your clips stay).

![The kits](../images/DrumKits87-2x.png)

<kbd>SAVE</kbd> once on a drum track saves the drum (on DRUM) or the kit (elsewhere) with the SAVE window, as a sound
is saved.

## The grid

Press <kbd>SEQ</kbd>, then SELECT to **CLIP**: on a drum track it is the **GRID**, the clip in sixteenth steps, a row a
drum.

![The GRID](../images/DrumGrid-2x.png)

1. Press a black key to pick the row (1 KICK to 9 DUCK).
2. Press white keys to put hits in or take them out on the sixteen steps shown.
3. Press <kbd>PLAY</kbd>.

Hold a step's key and turn the knobs to set that hit's VELOCITY, ACCENT, CHANCE and RATCHET. KNOB 1 moves through the
clip's steps.

## Euclid

ALGORITHM to **EUCLID** fills the row picked with a Euclidean rhythm: KNOB 1 HITS, KNOB 2 STEPS, KNOB 3 ROTATE, KNOB 4
the ROW. Each turn refills the row; the other rows stay.

![EUCLID](../images/DrumEuclid-2x.png)

> **Tip:** put hits on the DUCK row on each beat and turn HOME's DUCK knob up: the synth pumps around the beat, the
> classic sidechain sound, without a sound of its own.
