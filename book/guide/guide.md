# Guide

Strata turns the FM-1 into a wavetable synth, an 8-track looper and a drum machine. This page gets you playing and
shows every feature at a glance; each card links to its chapter of the Manual. Every button is on the
[cheat sheet](cheatsheet.md).

## Quick start

![HOME](../images/Home-2x.png)

1. **Install.** Open the site's **Install** page in Chrome or Edge, make a backup, then press **Find the FM-1** and
   **Write Strata** ([how](../manual/install.md)).
2. **Play.** Switch the FM-1 on. HOME shows the sound. Play the keys; <kbd>OCT−</kbd> and <kbd>OCT+</kbd> change the
   octave, and MASTER sets the level.
3. **Pick a sound.** Turn PRESETS to choose a preset from the list, then press <kbd>SAVE</kbd> twice to load it.
4. **Change it.** Press <kbd>EDIT</kbd>. Turn SELECT to choose a module (OSC A, OSC B, SUB, NOISE, FILTER, MATRIX),
   ALGORITHM to choose a page, and KNOB 1 to 4 to set the values. Hold <kbd>EDIT</kbd> and press <kbd>OCT+</kbd> to
   switch the module shown on.
5. **Save it.** Press <kbd>SAVE</kbd>. In the window, SAVE AS NEW is chosen for a sound you haven't saved before
   (<kbd>OCT−</kbd> and <kbd>OCT+</kbd> move the choice). Press <kbd>SAVE</kbd> to confirm, then <kbd>SAVE</kbd> again
   to keep the suggested name.

Hold <kbd>HOME</kbd> on any screen to see what every control does there. <kbd>SAVE</kbd> + <kbd>OCT−</kbd> undoes your
last change.

## How the buttons work

<div class="cols"></div>

| Press | Does |
| --- | --- |
| A menu button once | Opens its menu where you left it (pressed again: its first page) |
| Twice, quickly | Its second job (EDIT twice: the wavetable list; press it twice again to load the one chosen) |
| Held | Its layer, for as long as you hold it (FX held: the macros) |

| Press | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Button + <kbd>OCT+</kbd> | Switches its feature on, or adds |
| Button + <kbd>OCT−</kbd> | Switches it off, or removes |
| Button + <kbd>OCT−</kbd> held 5 s | Resets what the menu shows |

## Features

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>Sound</h3>
<p>Two wavetable oscillators on 54 tables, a sub, noise and a filter, with eight voices.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>EDIT</kbd> oscillators, filter, matrix</li><li><kbd>EDIT</kbd> twice: wavetable list</li><li><kbd>EDIT</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>/<kbd>OCT−</kbd>: module on/off</li><li><kbd>GLO</kbd>: voice mode, glide</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/sound.md">Manual: The sound</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Movement</h3>
<p>Two envelopes, two drawable LFOs and four macros, routed by an 8-slot matrix.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>ENV</kbd> twice: an envelope or macro onto a knob</li><li><kbd>LFO</kbd> twice: an LFO onto a knob</li><li><kbd>REC</kbd> held + a white key: the MOD layer</li><li><kbd>LFO</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>: add an LFO point</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/modulation.md">Manual: Envelopes, LFOs and modulation</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Effects</h3>
<p>A chain of up to eight effects from thirteen types, in any order.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>FX</kbd>: the chain</li><li><kbd>FX</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>: add the type chosen</li><li><kbd>FX</kbd> twice: open the effect</li><li><kbd>FX</kbd> + <kbd>OCT−</kbd>: remove or bypass</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/fx.md">Manual: Effects</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Performing</h3>
<p>Bend, mod wheel, macros and temporary tweaks from the knobs.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>GLO</kbd> held: PERFORM (bend, wheel, glide)</li><li><kbd>FX</kbd> held: MACROS and effect bypass</li><li><kbd>LFO</kbd> held: MOMENTARY changes</li><li><kbd>GLO</kbd> held + F3 (lowest key) taps: tap tempo</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/playing.md">Manual: Playing</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Scales, chords, ARP</h3>
<p>Fit the keys to a scale, play a chord on every key, arpeggiate it in time.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>SCL</kbd>: scale and root</li><li><kbd>SCL</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>: chords on</li><li><kbd>ARP</kbd> + <kbd>OCT+</kbd>: arpeggiator on</li><li><kbd>ARP</kbd>: style, groove, 16 steps</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/playing.md">Manual: Playing</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Looper</h3>
<p>Eight tracks of clips in eight scenes. Record takes and overdubs, then launch them.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>SEQ</kbd>: clips, mixer, song</li><li><kbd>REC</kbd>: record; <kbd>REC</kbd> twice: keep what you just played</li><li><kbd>SEQ</kbd> held: launch pad</li><li><kbd>SEQ</kbd> twice: projects</li><li>CLIPS: <kbd>OCT−</kbd>/<kbd>OCT+</kbd> scene, KNOB 4 track</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/looper.md">Manual: The looper</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Drums</h3>
<p>Set a track's TYPE to DRUMS: eight drum rows, seven models, presets, kits and a step grid.</p>
<ul><li><kbd>EDIT</kbd>: the drum and the kit</li><li><kbd>EDIT</kbd> twice: drum presets or kits</li><li><kbd>SEQ</kbd>, SELECT to CLIP: the grid</li><li>DUCK row: sidechain the synth</li></ul>
<a class="more" href="../manual/drums.md">Manual: The drums</a></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Presets and saving</h3>
<p>Eight starter sounds, INIT and 64 slots of your own, with undo.</p>
<ul><li>PRESETS, then <kbd>SAVE</kbd> twice: load</li><li><kbd>SAVE</kbd>: save or save as new</li><li><kbd>SAVE</kbd> twice: the browser</li><li><kbd>SAVE</kbd> + <kbd>OCT−</kbd> / <kbd>OCT+</kbd>: undo / redo</li></ul>
