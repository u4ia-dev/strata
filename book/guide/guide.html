Guide

Guide

Strata turns the FM-1 into a wavetable synth, an 8-track looper and a drum machine. This page gets you playing and shows every feature at a glance; each card links to its chapter of the Manual. Every button is on the cheat sheet.

Quick start

  1. Install. Open the site's Install page in Chrome or Edge, make a backup, then press Find the FM-1 and Write Strata (how).
  2. Play. Switch the FM-1 on. HOME shows the sound. Play the keys; OCT− and OCT+ change the octave, and MASTER sets the level.
  3. Pick a sound. Turn PRESETS to choose a preset from the list, then press SAVE twice to load it.
  4. Change it. Press EDIT. Turn SELECT to choose a module (OSC A, OSC B, SUB, NOISE, FILTER, MATRIX), ALGORITHM to choose a page, and KNOB 1 to 4 to set the values. Hold EDIT and press OCT+ to switch the module shown on.
  5. Save it. Press SAVE. In the window, SAVE AS NEW is chosen for a sound you haven't saved before (OCT− and OCT+ move the choice). Press SAVE to confirm, then SAVE again to keep the suggested name.

Hold HOME on any screen to see what every control does there. SAVE + OCT− undoes your last change.

How the buttons work

Press Does
A menu button once Opens its menu where you left it (pressed again: its first page)
Twice, quickly Its second job (EDIT twice: the wavetable list)
Held Its layer, for as long as you hold it (FX held: the macros)
Press Does
Button + OCT+ Switches its feature on, or adds
Button + OCT− Switches it off, or removes
Button + OCT− held 5 s Resets what the menu shows

Features

Sound

Two wavetable oscillators on 54 tables, a sub, noise and a filter, with eight voices.

  • EDIT oscillators, filter, matrix
  • EDIT twice: wavetable list
  • EDIT + OCT+/OCT−: module on/off
  • GLO: voice mode, glide
Manual: The sound

Movement

Two envelopes, two drawable LFOs and four macros, routed by an 8-slot matrix.

  • ENV twice: an envelope or macro onto a knob
  • LFO twice: an LFO onto a knob
  • REC held + a white key: the MOD layer
  • LFO + OCT+: add an LFO point
Manual: Envelopes, LFOs and modulation

Effects

A chain of up to eight effects from thirteen types, in any order.

  • FX: the chain
  • FX + OCT+: add the type chosen
  • FX twice: open the effect
  • FX + OCT−: remove or bypass
Manual: Effects

Performing

Bend, mod wheel, macros and temporary tweaks from the knobs.

  • GLO held: PERFORM (bend, wheel, glide)
  • FX held: MACROS and effect bypass
  • LFO held: MOMENTARY changes
  • GLO held + PLAY taps: tap tempo
Manual: Playing

Scales, chords, ARP

Fit the keys to a scale, play a chord on every key, arpeggiate it in time.

  • SCL: scale and root
  • SCL + OCT+: chords on
  • ARP + OCT+: arpeggiator on
  • ARP: style, groove, 16 steps
Manual: Playing

Looper

Eight tracks of clips in eight scenes. Record takes and overdubs, then launch them.

  • SEQ: clips, mixer, song
  • REC: record; REC twice: keep what you just played
  • SEQ held: launch pad
  • SEQ twice: projects
Manual: The looper

Drums

Set a track's TYPE to DRUMS: eight drum rows, seven models, presets, kits and a step grid.

  • EDIT: the drum and the kit
  • EDIT twice: drum presets or kits
  • SEQ, SELECT to CLIP: the grid
  • DUCK row: sidechain the synth
Manual: The drums

Presets and saving

Eight starter sounds, INIT and 64 slots of your own, with undo.

  • PRESETS, then SAVE twice: load
  • SAVE: save or save as new
  • SAVE twice: the browser
  • SAVE + OCT− / OCT+: undo / redo
Manual: Presets and saving

MIDI and USB

MIDI in over USB and the jack, MIDI out over USB, and USB audio to a computer.

  • SETTINGS > MIDI: channel, LEARN, program change
  • Track TYPE MIDI: notes out to a DAW
  • GLOBAL > CLOCK IN: follow MIDI clock
Manual: MIDI and USB

Settings

Themes, LED brightness, knob feel, MIDI, power saving.

  • HOME twice: SETTINGS
  • HOME held: help map
  • SETTINGS > WIPE: settings back to default
Manual: Settings, LEDs and DEBUG

Installing

Install from the web page, back up first, and go back at any time.

  • Install page: Find the FM-1, Write Strata
  • Return to official V15, or Felucca's installer
  • OCT− + OCT+ held 5 s: backup mode
Manual: Installing and recovery

When things go wrong

Messages explained, sounds that won't play, crashes and limits.

  • HOME held at power-on: start without the last sound
  • SETTINGS > WIPE: reset the settings
Manual: Troubleshooting
