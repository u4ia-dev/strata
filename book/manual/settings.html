Settings, LEDs and DEBUG

Settings, LEDs and DEBUG

Press HOME twice for the SETTINGS. ALGORITHM steps the pages; HOME leaves. Settings are kept by themselves a few seconds after you stop playing.

SETTINGS

Page Setting What it does
SCREEN COLOR Twelve themes: STRATA, CLASSIC, ICE, VIOLET, PAPER, AMBER, HI-CON, MONO, MOSS, DUSK, SAND, NEON
HELP Show a hint when a module opens (on)
LIGHTS LED brightness: OFF, LOW, MED, HIGH
PANEL ACCEL Knobs faster when turned quickly (on)
HOLD Time before a held button counts as held: 300–600 ms (400)
MIDI CHANNEL, LEARN, CLEAR, PROGRAM See MIDI and USB
POWER SHOW BATTERY OFF, on HOME, or on every screen
SCREEN OFF AFTER Darken the screen after 1, 5 or 15 minutes untouched; any control wakes it

The battery shows as four cells, or a bolt on USB power. Unconfirmed on hardware: the battery levels and the screen sleep.

LEDs

Every LED follows one code:

LED Means Examples
Off nothing there an empty clip, a key outside the scale
Dim something there a clip, a scale note, a drum, a menu button not shown
Medium in use the scale's root, an accented drum, the grid's playhead
Lit active a key held, a clip playing, the menu shown, the beat
Blinking waiting a launched clip, a count-in
Breathing recording, or a layer open a held or latched layer's button

REC is lit while recording and blinks while it waits for the bar. PLAY flashes on each beat while the transport runs. ARP is lit while the arpeggiator is on.

DEBUG

On any page, hold OCT−, F3 (the lowest key), G5 (the highest key) and GLO together for a quarter of a second. The four buttons are far apart on purpose, so DEBUG never opens by accident. While OCT− is down, F3 and G5 make no note and do nothing else (their release does nothing either). Anything you pressed before the fourth button landed does its usual job until then, and DEBUG takes it back when it opens: the octave and CLIPS scene OCT− stepped, MIDI LEARN's CC it took away, NAME IT's characters, a tap-tempo run, a drum-grid step (and the one-bar clip it made), a CLIPS launch (and the PLAY it started), a held note or hit (cut off, and taken out of what you were recording), and a latched chord F3 or G5 replaced (it comes back). Two limits: what a key did inside a layer (a MACROS bypass, the launch pad, SCL's root, a MOD source) stays, and a CLIPS launch that has already played is launched back from the start of the clip. (HOME held shows the help map only.) HOME leaves DEBUG. ALGORITHM steps its pages. They diagnose the hardware, and reset or test Strata:

  • BENCH: what the FM-1 measures (audio, CPU, screen, keys, tables, flash, stacks). RUN times the processor.
  • KEY SCAN: the panel's switches as they are read, live.
  • CORE 2: the FM-1's second core, off by default. ON takes effect at the next power-on; TEST runs a 30-second load while something plays. Unconfirmed on hardware.
  • TESTS: four test projects (below).
  • WIPE: turn KNOB 1; the screen asks. Press HOME twice to put every setting back to its default. Presets, projects and the drum library stay. Once, or any other button, keeps everything ("KEPT").

KEY SCAN draws the encoders' own lines as rings and names them, for example "PA5:9, PA5:10: KNOB 1". A cell with no key, button or knob that closes while you watch turns vermilion.

Test projects

Four built-in projects load and play by themselves, to put one part of Strata under load: use them to hear how a build copes, or to record the audio.

  1. Open DEBUG (OCT− + F3 + G5 + GLO held together). Turn ALGORITHM to TESTS.
  2. Turn KNOB 1 to choose a test. A moment later the screen asks, for example "TEST 2: FX?".
  3. Press HOME twice. The test loads and starts. Once, or another button, cancels.
  4. Turn KNOB 2 to stop it early. It also stops by itself after 60 to 90 seconds.
Test What it plays
1 VOICES Chords of 1, 2, 4 and then 8 notes at full velocity on SUPERSAW, PAD, VOWEL PAD and OTT GROWL in turn, with a filter envelope and an LFO moving the wavetable position.
2 FX A chain of eight effects that goes through all 13 types, every knob swept to both ends by LFOs and envelopes.
3 DRUMS Two drum tracks over all seven models: ratchets, chance, accents, each drum's effects, KIT ACCENT, the DUCK, its CURVE and COMP, and the duck on a held chord.
4 LOOPER All eight tracks (drums, bass, chords, ARP, a lead with recorded motion, speed changes, MIDI out), a SONG of five scenes with repeats, mutes, solos, volume and pan changing.
  • Loading a test first saves your open project, your settings and the sound you are playing, exactly as loading a project does.
  • Nothing from the test is ever saved. While one is loaded, automatic saving is off and the top line of every page says TEST · NOT SAVED (HOME shows the test's name).
  • A test ends when you load a project, make a NEW project, or restart. Stopping it, or letting it finish, leaves it loaded.
  • When it ends, the sound you had before the test comes back, so a sound you choose during a test is replaced when it ends. Save a sound you like first (SAVE), or it is lost. Tempo, time signature, CLOCK IN and the looper's settings come back too.

Unconfirmed on hardware.
