Limits

Limits

What Limit
Voices 8
Unison 16 copies per oscillator, within a shared budget
Wavetables 54 factory tables
Matrix 8 slots
Effects 8 in the chain; each type twice, DELAY and REVERB once
LFO points 16
ARP steps 16
Presets 64 of your own
Undo the last 8 changes
MIDI LEARN 32 mappings
Looper 8 tracks × 8 scenes; clips of 1–16 bars; 16 SONG slots
A project 2,048 notes, hits and moves
Projects 4
Drum library 64 drum presets, 16 kits

The processor. Eight voices with two oscillators and a filter fit comfortably. Big unison with every effect does not, so a guard steps in by itself: first the oldest notes lose unison copies, then the costliest effects get lighter, then a voice is released, each with a fade. Fewer unison copies, fewer effects (REVERB costs most) or fewer VOICES avoid it. Unconfirmed on hardware: the guard and the drums' cost under a full load.

Not yet: a separate sound for each synth track (all synth tracks play the one sound loaded), sending knob CCs or clock over MIDI, and putting the drums through the effects. MIDI out on the jack isn't possible: the FM-1's jack is an input only.
