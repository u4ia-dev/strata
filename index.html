Install Guide & manual Source

Before you install

Saving anything in Strata can overwrite Felucca's projects and samples (the same flash), and going back does not bring them back. Make a full flash backup first and keep it (how): it is the only way back to exactly what you had. Strata never writes the boot area, the calibration and trim data, Bluetooth pairing or the unit's own keys.

Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 on a USB data cable straight into it. Backups are made on your computer (the source's serve.py, or the Flipper Zero route), not on this site.

Good to know

The SysEx message F0 22 24 35 7D F7 on the FM-1's USB MIDI port (as on the official firmware) restarts it into backup mode; any program that can send SysEx can send it. Switching the FM-1 off and on returns to Strata. OCT− and OCT+ held 5 s do the same.

The USB serial console, the DEBUG menu (HOME held 5 s) and CORE 2 (off by default) are in every build.

Going back: M-VAVE's updater or the flasher's "Return to official V15" with M-VAVE's own file; Felucca's installer; or your backup by the Flipper Zero route.
