Strata hardware test

Runs the tests of FLASHING.md section 4 that the computer can do by itself, and guides the ones that need the panel, checking the result where it can. It plays notes and sends controllers and program changes over USB MIDI, reads Strata's console, and can listen to the FM-1's USB audio. It writes nothing to the FM-1's flash and sends no update. Two parts act on the FM-1 only when you press them: the test projects (section 3: they load a built-in project and play it) and the backup-mode buttons (section 7: the FM-1 leaves for backup mode and needs a power cycle).

0Before you start

  • Close the flasher's tab, M-Upgrade, DAWs and other MIDI apps: one app at a time can open the FM-1's ports.
  • On the FM-1, SETTINGS (HOME twice) > MIDI: CHANNEL ANY or 1, and PROGRAM ON (KNOB 4), so the page can switch sounds. With PROGRAM off the load test runs on the sound playing only.
  • The page changes the sound playing (it loads the starter sounds and moves the cutoff); it puts your preset back at the end if it came from a user slot. SAVE anything you want to keep first.
  • MASTER at a comfortable level: the load test plays chords of up to eight notes.

3The test projects (V0.87.1)

Each loads a built-in project on the FM-1 (tproj N), plays it for 60 to 90 s while this page records the FM-1's USB audio, then saves the sound and the run's figures in build/hwtest/ for the analysis (tools/hw_audio_check.py). Nothing of yours is touched: the projects are built in and read-only, and auto-save is off while one is loaded. Hands off the FM-1 and the volume while one runs. Needs the console connected and the FM-1's USB audio input (the device named "Strata for FM-1"; the browser asks to use the microphone once). Open the page through serve.py so it can save; without it each recording is offered as a download.

4Guided steps

Each step says what to do on the panel; the page watches the FM-1 and ticks what it saw. Mark a step when it is done, or say it did not work.

A. The five fixed effects' knobs (item 16)

For each of FX FILTER, BITCRUSH, TREMOLO, RING MOD and TAPE: on FX, KNOB 1 to the type, FX + OCT+ adds it, FX twice opens it; then turn any of its knobs while the note plays. The page checks that the sound's value follows the knob (V0.6: it stayed where the sound loaded it).

      C. The encoders and keys (item 5)

      Press Start, then turn each of the seven encoders both ways, slowly and quickly, and press every key once (on a page where the keys play), then Done.

      D. The keys and the screen while it draws (items 15 and 19)

      V0.87.1: 1. Go to HOME's first page (HOME, if you are elsewhere). 2. Hold HOME for 5 s: the bar fills and DEBUG opens (held on any other page it only shows the help map). It opens on BENCH. 3. Turn KNOB 2 (PEAKS): "THE PEAKS CLEARED". 4. Leave DEBUG (HOME once) and press Start here. For 25 s flick through pages (SELECT, ALGORITHM, the family buttons) and turn knobs on graph pages (an envelope, an LFO, the EQ, the FX FILTER, the chain view) while the page holds a chord. The page sends nothing to the console meanwhile, so it does not slow the FM-1's main loop. Afterwards it reads BENCH's longest key wait and frame.

      Two buttons say different things: It felt slow (the panel lagged: say where in the note) and I could not do the steps (DEBUG or BENCH did not open as described: say what the screen showed). A report line for either carries the button's own words.

      E. Switching off and on (item 1)

      Press Start, switch the FM-1 off, wait two seconds, switch it on. The page reconnects and reads how the start went. Do it three times.

      7Backup mode, three ways (V0.87.1, item 12)

      V0.87's way (OCT- + OCT+ held, or the SysEx soft key) left a black screen and an unknown USB device. Three ways are on trial; each writes nothing. Try one, power-cycle, then another. Each press makes the FM-1 leave for backup mode: its console drops, its audio and MIDI go away, and only a power cycle brings it back.

      • Way A: as V0.87 (the P33 reset, nothing else).
      • Way B: the watchdog stopped, the USB detached for about 100 ms, then the P33 reset.
      • Way C: as B, then a watchdog reset instead.
      1. Before pressing, look at Windows (Device Manager, Ports and Universal Serial Bus devices, or This PC) and write down what shows for the FM-1:
      2. Press one button (it asks first). The console should drop within a second or two if it works.
      3. Look at Windows again: a disk WL82 UBOOT1.00 (the backup-mode disk) is the good result; "Unknown USB device (Device Descriptor Request Failed)" is the bad one. Write it down:
      4. Power-cycle the FM-1 (off, wait, on) to come back, and connect the console again.
