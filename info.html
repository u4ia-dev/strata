STRATA

Strata turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a complete pocket instrument: a deep wavetable synth, an 8-track looper and a drum machine. Free software, built on Felucca. Package V0.875, SHA-256 c528eb13a860f3a5…

Before you install

Saving anything in Strata can overwrite Felucca's projects and samples (the same flash), and going back does not bring them back. Make a full flash backup first and keep it (how): it is the only way back to exactly what you had. Strata never writes the boot area, the calibration and trim data, Bluetooth pairing or the unit's own keys.

Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 on a USB data cable straight into it. Backups are made with a backup tool on your computer, not on this site.

Good to know

Backup mode: OCT− and OCT+ held 5 s, or the SysEx message F0 22 24 35 7D F7 on the FM-1's USB MIDI port (as on the official firmware). On the FM-1 tested, V0.87 left an unknown USB device; V0.875 works (tested on one FM-1). The FM-1 Transporter and a Flipper Zero enter backup mode at power-on, whatever the firmware does. Switching the FM-1 off and on returns to Strata.

The USB serial console, the DEBUG menu (OCT−, F3, G5 and GLO held together) and CORE 2 (off by default) are in every build.

Going back: M-VAVE's updater or the installer's "Return to official V15" with M-VAVE's own file; Felucca's installer; or your backup, written back in backup mode (how).
